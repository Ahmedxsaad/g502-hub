The mouse photo

The picture of the mouse is Logitech's product photo. It is not hosted on this site: your browser loads it directly from Logitech's website (logitechg.com), so that request goes to Logitech and their privacy policy applies to it. The app does the same: it downloads the photo once from logitechg.com the first time it starts. App screenshots in the project's repository show the app as it looks, with that photo in it.

Privacy

  • This site uses no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it has no forms or accounts.
  • Its fonts and images are served from this site, except the mouse photo described above.
  • Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • The install command downloads G502 Hub from GitHub; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The app itself runs entirely on your computer. Screen and audio are analysed locally for the live lighting effects and are never stored or sent anywhere. It collects no data; its only network access is the one-time photo download from logitechg.com.
